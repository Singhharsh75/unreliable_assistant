# Change Control

Any change to an approved process, specification, equipment, supplier, or computer system needs a change request (CR) before it is made.

Each CR is classified Minor, Moderate, or Major. Major changes need approval from the Change Control Board, which meets every Tuesday. Minor and Moderate changes are approved by the QA manager.
A Major change to a validated process also needs revalidation before the first lot made under it can be released.

Emergency changes may be made before approval only to protect product or patient safety. They must be documented within 24 hours and approved retrospectively within 5 working days.
Implementing a change without an approved CR is a deviation, see [[sop-deviation]]. Changes to suppliers also follow [[sop-supplier-qualification]].
