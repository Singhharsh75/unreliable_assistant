# Data Integrity (ALCOA+)

All GMP records must be ALCOA+: Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, plus Complete, Consistent, Enduring, and Available.

Paper corrections use a single line through the old value, so it stays readable, with the new value, initials, date, and a reason. Correction fluid and overwriting are forbidden.
Electronic records keep a full audit trail of every change, see [[policy-audit-trail]].

Shared user accounts are forbidden on GMP systems. Each person signs with their own credentials.
A data-integrity breach, such as backdating a record, is always classified at least Major, see [[sop-deviation]].
