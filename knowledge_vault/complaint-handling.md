# Customer Complaint Handling

Every complaint is logged within 1 working day of receipt with a complaint ID in the format CMP-NNNNNN.

Complaints are triaged as safety-related or quality-only. A safety-related complaint is escalated to the Head of Quality the same day and assessed for recall, see [[sop-recall]].
Investigation of a quality-only complaint must be completed within 30 calendar days.

The investigation checks the retained sample of the lot, the batch record, and any open deviations against the lot.
A complaint with a confirmed product defect requires a CAPA, see [[sop-capa]]. The customer receives a written response when the investigation closes.
Complaint data is trended monthly. Three complaints of the same type for one SKU in a quarter trigger a review by the product owner.
