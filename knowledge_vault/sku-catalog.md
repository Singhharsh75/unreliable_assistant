# SKU Catalog Conventions

Product SKUs use the format SKU-NNN (three digits, zero padded), for example SKU-099.
SKU-099 is a refrigerated product and follows [[sop-cold-chain]].
SKU-120 is shelf-stable at room temperature.
Discontinued SKUs keep their number and are never reassigned.
