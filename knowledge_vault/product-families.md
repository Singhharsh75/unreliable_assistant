# Product Families

SKU-099 (Cardiolex 5 mg injection) is refrigerated, stored between 2 °C and 8 °C, with a shelf life of 18 months.
SKU-120 (Dermasol cream 50 g) is shelf-stable at room temperature, with a shelf life of 36 months.
SKU-145 (Pulmovent inhaler) is stored at room temperature, with a shelf life of 24 months.
SKU-210 (Glycobal 500 mg tablets) is stored at room temperature, with a shelf life of 48 months.
SKU-305 (Vaxigen vaccine 0.5 mL) is refrigerated and must never be frozen, with a shelf life of 12 months.

Refrigerated SKUs follow [[sop-cold-chain]]. Room-temperature SKUs follow [[sop-warehouse-storage]].
SKU-077 was discontinued in 2024; its number is kept and never reassigned, see [[catalog-sku]].
