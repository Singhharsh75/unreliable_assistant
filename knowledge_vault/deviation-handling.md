# Deviation Handling

A deviation is any departure from an approved procedure or specification.
Deviations are classified Minor, Major, or Critical within 24 hours of detection.
Critical deviations place every affected lot on HOLD immediately.
A deviation is closed only after root-cause analysis and CAPA approval.
