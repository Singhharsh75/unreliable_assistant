# Warehouse Storage

Room-temperature products are stored between 15 °C and 25 °C with relative humidity below 60%.
Refrigerated products are stored per [[sop-cold-chain]].

Warehouse locations are mapped for temperature every 2 years and after any change to the building or HVAC system.
QUARANTINE, REJECTED, and RECALLED stock is kept in a physically separate, locked area. Only warehouse staff with the quarantine role can move stock in or out of it.

A full stock count is done once a year. A count difference above 0.5% for any SKU is investigated as a deviation, see [[sop-deviation]].
