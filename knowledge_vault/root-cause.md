# Root-Cause Analysis

Root-cause analysis (RCA) is required for every Major and Critical deviation and every confirmed out-of-specification result, see [[sop-oos]].
The RCA report is due within 15 working days of deviation classification.

Approved methods are the 5 Whys, the fishbone (Ishikawa) diagram, and fault-tree analysis. Critical deviations must use at least two methods.
The RCA team includes the process owner, a QA representative, and at least one operator from the affected shift.

"Human error" is not accepted as a root cause on its own. The report must state why the error was possible, for example unclear instructions or missing training, see [[sop-training]].
When no root cause can be found, the report is labelled "root cause not determined" and the CAPA must include extra monitoring for 6 months, see [[sop-capa]].
