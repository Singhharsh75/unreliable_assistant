# Training and Qualification

Staff may perform a GMP task only after training on the current version of its procedure is recorded.

New procedures and revised procedures must be trained before their effective date. The training window is 14 days.
Operators are requalified on aseptic technique every 6 months. Qualified Persons complete 20 hours of continuing education per year.

Training records are kept for the length of employment plus 10 years, see [[policy-record-retention]].
A task performed by someone without a valid training record is a deviation, see [[sop-deviation]]. RCA reports that cite human error must check the training record, see [[sop-root-cause]].
