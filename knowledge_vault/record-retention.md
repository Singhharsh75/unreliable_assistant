# Record Retention

Batch records and QA sign-off records are kept for 1 year after the lot's expiry date, or 5 years after release, whichever is longer.
Shipping and distribution records are kept for 5 years.
Training records are kept for the length of employment plus 10 years.
Complaint and recall records are kept for 10 years.

Records may be destroyed only after the retention period ends and QA approves the destruction list.
Records under a legal hold are never destroyed, whatever their retention period.
Electronic records must stay readable for the whole retention period, including after a system is retired.
