# Cold-Chain Storage

Temperature-sensitive products are stored between 2 °C and 8 °C.
Any excursion above 8 °C for more than 30 minutes is a deviation, see [[sop-deviation]].
Temperature loggers are read before a cold-chain lot is released, see [[sop-lot-release]].
