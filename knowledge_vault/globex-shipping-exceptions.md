# Shipping Exceptions (tenant_globex only)

tenant_globex accepts lots with at least 4 months of remaining shelf life, instead of the standard 6 months, under its written agreement with us.
Cold-chain deliveries to tenant_globex must arrive within 36 hours instead of 48.
Globex delivery issues are reported to the Globex logistics desk within 4 hours.
This note is visible only to tenant_globex.
