# Supplier Qualification

Only approved suppliers may supply materials that touch the product. Approved suppliers are listed in the Approved Supplier List (ASL).

A new supplier is approved after a questionnaire, a quality agreement, and, for active ingredients and primary packaging, an on-site audit.
Approved suppliers are re-audited every 3 years, or every year if they had a Critical audit finding.

Suppliers are scored quarterly on on-time delivery and rejected deliveries. A supplier with more than 3 rejected deliveries in 12 months is moved to conditional status and every delivery gets full testing, see [[sop-incoming-inspection]].
Removing a supplier from the ASL or adding a new one is a change under [[sop-change-control]].
