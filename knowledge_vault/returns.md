# Returned Goods

Returned product is placed in QUARANTINE on arrival and is never put straight back into saleable stock.

Returned goods can be returned to stock only if all of these are true: the packaging is unopened, the product stayed within its storage conditions the whole time, and more than 12 months of shelf life remain.
Cold-chain products can never be returned to stock, because their storage history outside our control cannot be verified. They are destroyed.

The decision to return to stock is made by QA and recorded against the original lot number.
Product returned because of a complaint is handled under [[sop-complaint-handling]] and kept as evidence until that complaint is closed.
