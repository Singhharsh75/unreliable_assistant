# Incoming Material Inspection

Every incoming delivery is placed in QUARANTINE on receipt and cannot be used until QC releases it.

QC checks the delivery against the purchase order, the supplier's certificate of analysis, and the label. Active ingredients are identity-tested on every container.
Other materials from approved suppliers are sampled using the square-root rule: sample √n + 1 containers, where n is the number of containers delivered.
Materials from a supplier on conditional status are fully tested, see [[sop-supplier-qualification]].

Released material gets a green RELEASED label. Rejected material gets a red REJECTED label and is returned or destroyed within 30 days.
Cold-chain materials are checked for temperature excursions during transport before they are moved into storage, see [[sop-cold-chain]].
