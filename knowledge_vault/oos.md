# Out-of-Specification Results

An out-of-specification (OOS) result is a test result outside its approved specification.

Phase I is a laboratory investigation by the analyst and the lab supervisor, completed within 3 working days. It checks for calculation errors, instrument problems, and sample preparation errors.
If Phase I finds a clear laboratory error, the result is invalidated and the test is repeated.
If not, Phase II, a full investigation including manufacturing, starts and must finish within 20 working days.

Retesting to "test into compliance" is forbidden. A confirmed OOS result means the lot is rejected unless QA documents a scientific justification.
Every confirmed OOS needs root-cause analysis and a CAPA, see [[sop-root-cause]] and [[sop-capa]].
