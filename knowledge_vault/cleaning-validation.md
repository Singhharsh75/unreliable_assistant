# Cleaning and Line Clearance

Shared equipment is cleaned between products using a validated cleaning procedure.
The acceptance limit for carry-over is 10 ppm of the previous product's active ingredient, or 1/1000 of its lowest therapeutic dose, whichever is lower.

Cleaned equipment carries a CLEAN status label valid for 72 hours. After 72 hours it must be cleaned again before use.
Before a new lot starts, the operator performs a line clearance: remove all materials, labels, and documents from the previous lot, and a second person verifies it.

A failed swab test is a deviation, see [[sop-deviation]]. The cleaning procedure is revalidated whenever a new product is added to shared equipment, under [[sop-change-control]].
