# In-Process Checks

In-process checks (IPCs) confirm a lot stays within limits while it is being made. All IPC results are step 2 of lot release, see [[sop-lot-release]].

Standard IPCs for tablets: weight every 15 minutes, hardness every 30 minutes, and disintegration once per hour.
Standard IPCs for liquids: pH and fill volume every 20 minutes.

A single result outside limits stops the line. The operator retests once with a fresh sample. If the retest is also outside limits, the shift supervisor raises a deviation, see [[sop-deviation]].
An IPC instrument must have a valid calibration on the day of use, see [[sop-calibration]]. Results from an instrument found out of calibration are invalid back to its last passing calibration.
