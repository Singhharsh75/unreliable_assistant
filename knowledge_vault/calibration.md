# Equipment Calibration

Every measuring instrument that affects product quality has a calibration ID in the format CAL-NNNN and a calibration interval.
Default intervals: balances every 6 months, temperature probes every 12 months, pH meters every 3 months.

A calibration label on the instrument shows the last and next due date. An instrument past its due date must not be used.
If an instrument fails calibration, maintenance tags it OUT OF SERVICE and QA assesses every result measured since the last passing calibration.
That impact assessment must be finished within 5 working days. Affected lots are placed on HOLD until it is finished.

Temperature loggers used for cold-chain storage are temperature probes and follow the 12-month interval, see [[sop-cold-chain]].
