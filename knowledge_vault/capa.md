# Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)

A CAPA is opened for every Major or Critical deviation, every confirmed out-of-specification result, and every complaint with a confirmed product defect.
Each CAPA has one owner, a due date, and an effectiveness check. Minor deviations need a CAPA only if the same root cause recurs three times within 12 months.

The CAPA plan must be approved by QA within 10 working days of the root-cause report, see [[sop-root-cause]].
Corrective actions fix the detected problem; preventive actions stop it happening on other lines, products, or sites.

Due dates: Critical CAPAs are due within 30 calendar days, Major CAPAs within 60 calendar days.
A due date can be extended once, by up to 30 days, with a written justification approved by the QA manager.

The effectiveness check runs 90 days after the last action is closed. If the problem recurs during those 90 days, the CAPA is reopened.
A deviation can only be closed after its CAPA is approved, see [[sop-deviation]].
