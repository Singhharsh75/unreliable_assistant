# Stability Testing

Stability studies confirm a product stays within specification for its full shelf life.

The first three commercial lots of every new product enter the stability program. After that, one lot per SKU per year is added.
Long-term samples are tested at 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, and 24 months, then yearly until the end of shelf life.
Refrigerated products are stored at 5 °C ± 3 °C for long-term testing. Room-temperature products are stored at 25 °C ± 2 °C and 60% relative humidity.

A stability result outside specification is an out-of-specification result, see [[sop-oos]], and is reported to the Head of Quality within 2 working days.
Shelf life can only be extended after real-time data covers the new period, and the extension is a change under [[sop-change-control]].
