# Environmental Monitoring

Production rooms are graded A to D. Grade A is the cleanest and is used for aseptic filling.

Viable air samples are taken in every Grade A and B room during every shift, and in Grade C rooms weekly. Grade D rooms are sampled monthly.
Each result is compared with an alert level and an action level. An alert-level result triggers extra sampling for 3 consecutive days. An action-level result is a deviation, see [[sop-deviation]].

Room differential pressure is logged continuously. A pressure drop lasting more than 15 minutes in a Grade A or B room stops aseptic work in that room.
Any lot filled in a room during an action-level excursion is placed on HOLD until QA completes an impact assessment.
